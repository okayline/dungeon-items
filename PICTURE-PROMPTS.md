# Picture prompts

The prompts used for each chapter's pictures, one entry per chapter, newest first. Dates are Hawaii time (HST).

Each chapter gets 3 pictures. One prompt each, one thing at a time:

1. **Setting and mood** (Phoenix-1.0): the place, the light, the feel.
2. **Subject and action** (Flux-2-Klein-9B): one character doing one thing.
3. **Subject and action** (Flux-2-Klein-9B): one character doing one thing.

What worked in the tests: short plain sentences; concrete things you can point at; always say what the light is like; Flux keeps small details, Phoenix keeps the mood and drops small things.

## 2026-10-27, chapter 10 (stories id 13): No Mercy, the Sewer

Tested 2026-10-28 in the Cloudflare playground on all four models (no pictures saved yet). Flux got all three parts both times, including the pale figure; Phoenix and Lucid got the mood and dropped the pale figure.

1. Setting (Phoenix):
   > A long concrete drainage tunnel under a hospital, wet floor, rusted pipes along the ceiling, one flickering yellow light far down the corridor, deep shadow on both sides.
2. Subject (Flux):
   > A man in a leather vest crouches in a dark concrete tunnel, lining up glass bottles with burning rags along the floor in a straight careful row. He looks calm and focused. Orange firelight on wet walls.
3. Subject (Flux):
   > A young woman in a torn red jacket stands in a dim sewer tunnel holding a shotgun, tired but steady. Far ahead in the green-tinted dark, a pale thin figure with its head bowed stands very still.
